Reading it

Each node's interval is split among its children. Height is the child's probability under p; width is its surprisal under q, −log q. With p = q the boxes of a node add up to its entropy in area. With different measures, they add up to its cross-entropy.

For ChatML text, blue shows the user turn, green the assistant turn, and purple the assistant's thinking.

A pixel can show several sibling boundaries until you zoom in.
