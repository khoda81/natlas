Reading it

Each node's interval is split among its children. Height is the child's probability under p; width is its surprisal under q, −log q. With p = q the boxes of a node add up to its entropy in area; otherwise to its cross-entropy, and color shows ln(p/q).

Gray tails are children not yet listed, drawn at the least width they can have. Sub-pixel children merge into borderless runs without changing the tree.
